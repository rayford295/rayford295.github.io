Papers record conclusions. They rarely record how you got there — the papers you
misread first, the question you couldn't phrase for three weeks, the afternoon a
baseline broke and taught you more than the result you eventually reported.

This page is for that second record. Reading notes, research reflections, essays,
and occasional non-academic writing about football, cards, and whatever else keeps
a Ph.D. student intact.

Two rules I'm setting for myself:

1. **Write to think, not to perform.** A note that is honest and unfinished is
   worth more than a post that sounds finished and says nothing.
2. **Publish before it's polished.** Otherwise nothing ever gets published.

Some pieces here were first written in Chinese for my WeChat official account —
those are linked out rather than duplicated. Everything else lives on this site.
